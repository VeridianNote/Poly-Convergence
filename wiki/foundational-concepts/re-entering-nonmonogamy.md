# Re-entering non-monogamy

If you've been in a non-monogamous relationship before and life closed things down for a while (stress, logistics, a new baby, a job change, just needing a break), wanting to re-open doesn't make you a unicorn hunter. It makes you someone whose circumstances changed.

This is a topic that gets remarkably little attention, even in poly spaces. There's plenty of guidance for people opening up for the first time. There's almost nothing for people coming back.
