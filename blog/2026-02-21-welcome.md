Welcome to Poly Convergence — a community-driven site dedicated to examining relationship advice with evidence and lived experience.

<!-- truncate -->

This site exists because misinformation in the relationship advice space can cause real harm. Our goal is to provide a space where claims can be examined against documented facts, research, and the experiences of real people.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Blog posts** examining specific claims and providing evidence-based analysis
- **A wiki** with educational content organized by topic
- **Community stories** from people willing to share their experiences

## How to Get Involved

This is an open-source, community-driven project. If you want to contribute content, share your story, or help improve the site, check out our [Contributing guide](/contributing).

All contributions are made through GitHub pull requests and are reviewed before publishing.
